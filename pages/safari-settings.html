How to use

  • In Safari, tap the ᴀA (or puzzle) button in the address bar → JP Reader.
  • Highlight this page colours Japanese words; tap a word for its meaning and to make a card.
  • Subtitles for this page's video: start the video, then open your .srt/.vtt/.ass file in the panel. Use Big video instead of the player's fullscreen: iPhone's own fullscreen player can't show extra subtitles.
  • Cards you make are added to Anki by Firefox on its next sync.
