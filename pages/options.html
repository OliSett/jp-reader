JP Reader settings

Three things to set up once: connect Anki, pick your Japanese decks, import the dictionary.

1 · Connect Anki

Install the AnkiConnect add-on in Anki (Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons → code 2055492159), restart Anki, and keep it open. The first time you connect, Anki shows a pop-up asking to allow this extension — click Yes.

2 · Your Japanese words

Connect Anki first.

    

      

      

      

      

        

    

    
  


  

    
3 · Dictionary (JMdict)

    
Splits Japanese text into words, handles conjugations, and adds readings and meanings to your cards. Download the newest jmdict-eng-…json.zip (or .tgz) from
      jmdict-simplified releases (a free, open-source export of JMdict) and choose it below. You only need to do this once.

    

  


  

    
4 · Frequency list (recommended)

    
Ranks every word by how common it is, so you can learn the most useful words first. Download Jiten's list in one click, or import any
      Yomitan frequency dictionary (.zip, e.g. JPDB, BCCWJ, Innocent Corpus) or a CSV/TSV word list sorted most-common-first.

📱 Phone (iPhone app)

    
Use JP Reader on your phone: books, videos, highlighting and word lists, all with your Anki words. Cards you make on the phone are added to Anki here the next time Firefox syncs (every 5 minutes, while Anki is open).

Recommendations (Jiten.moe)

    
Title lists and vocabulary come from Jiten.moe's free public API. A key is optional; it raises the rate limit so scans run about twice as fast (Jiten → Settings → Advanced → API key).
